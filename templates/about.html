About the product

What is ScrapIQ?

ScrapIQ is an intelligent, educational web scraping assistant that turns public HTML pages into structured insights you can explore, archive, and export.

Mission

Help students and analysts quickly understand how a page is built: semantics, metadata, media references, and lightweight SEO signals — without writing custom parsers for every assignment.

Responsible use

Only scrape sites you own or are permitted to access. Respect robots.txt, terms of service, and rate limits. ScrapIQ is a learning tool, not a bypass for protections.

How it works

Flask receives your URL, requests fetches the response, and BeautifulSoup with the lxml parser walks the DOM. Text is analyzed with regular expressions and simple NLP-style token counting.

Persistence

Each successful scrape is stored in SQLite as JSON for fast replays, history search, and pandas-powered CSV exports for spreadsheets or grading rubrics.
